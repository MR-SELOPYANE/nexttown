1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 
HITCH SA is a cutting-edge digital mobility platform that reimagines the classic hitchhiking 
experience, turning it into a safe, secure, and tech-savvy transportation ecosystem. 
The app connects: 
* Travelers looking for budget-friendly transport 
* Drivers with extra seats to fill 
* Truck operators seeking return loads 
* Parcel senders and transporters 
* Daily commuters 
* School transport communities 
* Airlines with vacant seats 
* Private aviation operators 
* Logistics companies 
With features like identity verification, facial recognition, vehicle validation, payment 
processing, GPS tracking, and safety monitoring, the platform ensures a secure travel 
experience for everyone involved.

8. USER REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS 
Travelers 
To register, users must provide: 
• South African ID 
• Passport (for foreign nationals) 
• Selfie for verification 
• Mobile number 
• Email address 
The system will: 
• Verify the ID. 
• Confirm face recognition. 
• Validate the cellphone number. 
• Perform fraud checks. 
Drivers 
To register, drivers must provide: 
• ID document 
• Driver's license 
• Vehicle registration 
• Vehicle license disc 
• Photos of the vehicle 
• Proof of address 
• Bank account details 
The system will: 
• Validate the driver's license. 
• Confirm vehicle ownership. 
• Check for license expiry. 
• Verify the driver's identity. 
• Approve the registration.
